Privacy Policy

At Nutrivana, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse or place an order on Nutrivana, we collect essential information required to fulfill your order safely:

  • Full Name and Delivery Address (City, Area, Postal Code).
  • Mobile Phone Number and WhatsApp Number (for shipping updates and courier dispatch).
  • Email Address (for order receipts and dispatch confirmation).
  • Order details and transaction history.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used for the following purposes:

  • Processing and delivering your order to your doorstep.

Nutrivana values your trust. We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing companies.
