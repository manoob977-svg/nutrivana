Refund Policy

We want you to be 100% happy with your order. Please read our refund and exchange policy carefully.

1. When Can You Ask for a Refund or Exchange?

  • You can ask for a refund or exchange if the product is defective or if you received the wrong product.
  • You must contact us within 3 days after you receive your parcel.
  • If you contact us after 3 days of parcel delivery, we will not accept the refund/exchange request.

2. What You Have to Do:

  • First, message our team on WhatsApp (+92 334 7000322) and send pictures or videos showing the problem clearly.
  • If our team approves the claim, you will send the product back to Nutrivana's official address.
  • Once we receive the returned product, we will dispatch your new replacement product or process your refund immediately.

3. Courier Charges:

  • We will pay a maximum of Rs. 200 for the return courier charges.
  • You must send us the courier receipt/slip as proof.
  • If you don't send the slip proof, we will not pay for the courier return cost.

4. How You Will Get the Refund:

Refund will be sent through Bank Transfer, EasyPaisa, or JazzCash within 3 to 7 working days after we receive and inspect the returned product.

💡 5. Important Note:

If you don't like the personal taste or flavor of the product, we cannot give a refund or exchange. Organic superfood taste preferences are subjective.
